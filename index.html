01 — The fleet

Two Cessnas. Kept right.

Well-equipped aircraft that are rigorously maintained. Both are ADS-B Out compliant and live in the West T-hangars at New Castle.

Cessna 172 Skyhawk

N2918E

A Skyhawk with a little extra muscle: its 180 HP engine was overhauled in January 2017. Easy to fly, easy to love, and right at home on local hops and weekend breakfast runs.

Cessna 182 Skylane

02 — Membership

Flying that actually pencils out.

We have 27 active flyers on our roster. Members share the cost of keeping two great airplanes flying, and the hourly rate tracks the price of fuel rather than a rental markup.

  • Cessna 172 SkyhawkPer hour · wet · tach time$118/hr
  • Cessna 182 SkylanePer hour · wet · tach time$171/hr
  • Monthly dues$197/mo
  • Membership feeOne time$500
  • Bond purchaseRefunded when you leave the club$3,000

Rates as of January 2026. The hourly rate is pegged to the price of fuel at ILG — when fuel was about $5/gallon, it was $95/hr for the C172 and $135/hr for the C182.

03 — About the club

Logging hours since 1973.

Del-Penn Flyers Inc. was organized in 1973 for the purpose of safe, economical flying. Five founding members and a Piper TriPacer started it all at Toughkenamon airport — and the club celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2023.

Club logbook · Del-Penn Flyers Inc.
DateAircraftRemarks
1973Piper TriPacerClub organized at Toughkenamon airport by five founding members.
1975Cessna 120A taildragger joins the line.
SinceCessna Skylane · Mooney M20 · Piper 180 · Piper Archer · two Skyhawks · two Cardinal RGsA long line of club airplanes, each shared by members who split the cost and the fun.
2023—Fifty years in the air: the club celebrates its 50th anniversary.
